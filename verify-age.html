To request age review, upload a clear image of the photo page/front of a government-issued identity document and a recent selfie. Accepted image formats: JPG, PNG or WebP, maximum 8 MB each.

Your evidence is private and is only made available to authorised Market administrators for review. Do not upload someone else's document.
